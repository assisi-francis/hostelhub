Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get most often.

HostelHub is open to students looking for affordable accommodation and landlords or property managers wanting to list their spaces. Anyone 18+ can create an account and start exploring listings.

Browsing and searching for hostels is completely free. Students can message landlords at no cost. Landlords pay a small listing fee only when they publish a property.

Search listings by location, filter by price and amenities, then click "Book Now" on any listing you like. You'll be guided through a secure checkout and receive a confirmation email instantly.
